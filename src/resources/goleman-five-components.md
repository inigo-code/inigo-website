## Where the idea comes from

- **1990:** Psychologists **Peter Salovey and John Mayer** coined the term *emotional intelligence* (EI): the ability to perceive, understand, use and manage emotions in oneself and others.
- **1995:** **Daniel Goleman** popularised it in *Emotional Intelligence: Why It Can Matter More Than IQ*.
- **1998:** Goleman applied it to the workplace in *Working with Emotional Intelligence* and the *Harvard Business Review* article "What Makes a Leader?", setting out five components.

Keep the two traditions apart in exam answers: Salovey and Mayer describe EI as an **ability**; Goleman's model is a **mixed model** that includes traits and competencies.

## The five components

| Component | What it means | What it looks like at work |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-awareness** | Recognising your own emotions, strengths, weaknesses and values, and their effect on others | Asking for feedback; knowing which situations make you impatient |
| **Self-regulation** | Controlling or redirecting disruptive impulses and moods; thinking before acting | Staying calm in a crisis; not sending the angry email |
| **Motivation** | A passion to work for reasons beyond money or status; persistence and optimism | Setting stretch goals; bouncing back after a failure |
| **Empathy** | Understanding the emotional make-up of other people and treating them accordingly | Noticing a team member is struggling; adapting your message to your audience |
| **Social skill** | Managing relationships and building networks; finding common ground | Leading change; resolving conflict; persuading others |

The first three are about **managing yourself**; the last two are about **managing relationships**.

## Why it matters for managers

- **Leadership:** Goleman argued that, among senior leaders, EI distinguishes outstanding performers more than technical skill or IQ alone.
- **Teams:** emotionally intelligent managers handle conflict and stress better, which affects the whole team's climate.
- **Customer-facing work:** empathy and self-regulation shape service quality.
- **Limits:** critics argue that some EI measures overlap with personality traits and that claims about its importance were sometimes overstated. A good answer mentions this.
